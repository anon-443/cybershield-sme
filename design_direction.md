# CyberShield Design Direction: Signal Observatory

CyberShield will use a **signal observatory** visual system rather than a generic dark cyber dashboard. The interface will feel like a calm, high-trust operations instrument: warm ivory surfaces, deep ink typography, controlled cobalt and electric-lime signal accents, slim evidence rules, and carefully spaced card stacks. This creates a clear visual identity distinct from the supplied marketplace, assessment, events, rental, and retail examples.

The public experience will lead with a bounded product statement, a single dominant domain-assessment surface, and a readable explanation of exactly what is observed. It will use one expressive display face for product moments and a compact technical sans for data. Headings will stay concise. Supporting sentences will use sensible maximum widths and natural paragraph breaks rather than artificial line fragmentation.

The workspace and report will use an editorial ledger structure: summary metrics first, decisions second, evidence third. Visual motion will remain purposeful and brief—chart lines drawing in, score rings filling, and cards entering with small vertical transitions. Large blank regions without content will be removed; spacing will support hierarchy rather than act as decoration.
